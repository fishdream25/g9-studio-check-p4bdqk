# Gases

## Objectives

*Describe gas behaviour with the ideal gas law and Dalton's law.*

## The ideal gas law

The ideal gas law relates pressure, volume and temperature: $PV = nRT$.

| Quantity | Symbol | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| Pressure | P | atm |
| Volume | V | L |
| Temperature | T | K |

## Partial pressure

In a mixture, each gas contributes its partial pressure: $P_{total} = P_A + P_B$. Water vapour is $\ce{H2O}$.
